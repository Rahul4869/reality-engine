## The product is a feeling
- It happens to YOU: first person, inside the scene, my body. People must relate and feel.
- Awe, dread, scale, wonder, shock - with witty, relatable humour. Never a lecture.
- Weird, mind-piercing titles. Edgy, never monotonous. A reason to come back.
- Works for viewers who do not care about science: everyday words, few numbers, the promise shown first (the viewer's seat, department D13).
- Never AI slop, never a repeated template, never one more channel in a crowded pool: a format is data, and the formats rotate.
- Cheap models never make taste calls: strong judges see every candidate.

## The long video has a format (`whatifs/formats.py`; the shares are settings Strategy can move)
- **F1 "The day it changed"** (70 of 100): no courtroom. One law of nature is different today - the impossible picture, your morning, your street, the world, the last
  consequence, the question "would you keep it?" - told by two voices, a guide and a foil. The show's devices are optional in it.
- **F2 "The trial"** (30 of 100, an experiment arm): the show **Reality on Trial** the owner designed on 9 Nov 2026 - a science what-if is put on trial in a

## The Short is a product of its own (`whatifs/shorts2.py`, the Shorts Studio D14; never a cut-down, never a trial)
- One guide voice speaking to you; nobody on screen; every second an animated clip; no plate at either end; the last frame is the first frame (it loops).
- **S1 "The second it changed"**: the impossible picture, two consequences, a question that hands back to the first picture (20 to 35 seconds).
- **S2 "What would you do?"**: you gain or lose one power, it turns personal, the price, your own choice (20 to 45 seconds).
- **S3 "The whole thing in 30 seconds"**: one big idea as one continuous camera move that returns to where it started (20 to 35 seconds).
- Before any picture is paid the viewer's seat for a Short measures it: a hook that ends inside 3 seconds of the real voice, one number at most, no expert word,
  no ask, unlike the last ten Shorts (checks `short_open`, `short_plain`, `short_shape`, `short_kept`). A Short that fails is not made.

## Every topic starts as a Short (`whatifs/ladder.py`, Growth and Learning D10)
- Three Shorts a week, twelve a month. Each Short's real numbers are read (YouTube delivers them 48 to 72 hours late); when a long video is about to start,
  code promotes the best Short of the last 30 days whose topic has no long video yet, and the long video is about that topic (check `topic_ladder`).
- No promoted Short and no override on record = no long video. A Short at three times the channel's usual views gets one follow-up Short within 48 hours.
- Until batch V2c-b2b the long video is about the proven topic but opens with its own pictures; the Short's footage is kept for that opening.

## A Short reaches three platforms natively (`whatifs/release.py`, Publishing D9)
- One master file, three uploads of their own: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels (a Trial Reel: non-followers first), Facebook Reels (90 seconds at most). Each gets its own words,
  built by code from the Short's script; none carries an ask, a courtroom word or a link.
- Every setting code can set is a row of the release sheet; after the upload each platform is read back and each row gets a receipt (check `release_sheet`).
  The link from a Short to its long video can only be set by hand in YouTube Studio: it is on the sheet as the owner's row and is never called done by the machine.
