## The show
A science what-if is put on trial in a **Toybox Noir** courtroom (stop-motion puppet look; Settings `ART_STYLE` = `toybox_noir`).

| Character | Role | Must always be true |
|---|---|---|
| Inspector Vale | calm noir detective | investigates the case; dry, precise |
| Dr. Grimm | theatrical mad scientist | his Prototype demonstration always fails (contract check `grimm_prototype`) |
| Nana Rosa | blunt grandma judge | her verdict opens with "Here's what's true" (contract check `verdict_line`) |
| the Archivist | narrator and evidence keeper | science claims enter as Exhibits with real sources (contract check `exhibits`) |

## The product is a feeling
- It happens to YOU: first person, inside the scene, my body. People must relate and feel.
- Awe, dread, scale, wonder, shock - with witty, relatable humour. Never a lecture.
- Weird, mind-piercing titles. Edgy, never monotonous. Characters to fall in love with. A reason to come back.
- Works for viewers who do not care about science. Tier-1 audiences, sponsor-ready.
- Never AI slop, never a repeated template, never one more channel in a crowded pool.
- Cheap models never make taste calls: strong judges see every candidate.

## Formats
- Long episodes (the trial), each engineered to break out; standalone daily Shorts (mini-trials); bridge Shorts and Reels cut from the long video.
- Spin-offs: the Case Files countdown and the monthly Deep Trial.
- Sound: per-world beds and story-tied events; ASMR only as touches.
