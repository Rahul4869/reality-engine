## The product is a feeling
- It happens to YOU: first person, inside the scene, my body. People must relate and feel.
- Awe, dread, scale, wonder, shock - with witty, relatable humour. Never a lecture.
- Weird, mind-piercing titles. Edgy, never monotonous. A reason to come back.
- Works for viewers who do not care about science: everyday words, few numbers, the promise shown first (the viewer's seat, department D13).
- Never AI slop, never a repeated template, never one more channel in a crowded pool: a format is data, and the formats rotate.
- Cheap models never make taste calls: strong judges see every candidate.

## The Short is a product of its own (`whatifs/shorts2.py`, the Shorts Studio D14; never a cut-down, never a trial)
- One guide voice speaking to you; nobody on screen; every second an animated clip; no plate at either end; the last frame is the first frame (it loops).
- **S1 "The second it changed"**: the impossible picture, two consequences, a question that hands back to the first picture (20 to 35 seconds).
- **S2 "What would you do?"**: you gain or lose one power, it turns personal, the price, your own choice (20 to 45 seconds).
- **S3 "The whole thing in 30 seconds"**: one big idea as one continuous camera move that returns to where it started (20 to 35 seconds).
- Before any picture is paid the viewer's seat for a Short measures it: a hook that ends inside 3 seconds of the real voice, one number at most, no expert word,
  no ask, unlike the last ten Shorts (checks `short_open`, `short_plain`, `short_shape`, `short_kept`). A Short that fails is not made.
