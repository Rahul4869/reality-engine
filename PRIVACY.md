# Privacy Policy – Reality Engine

Reality Engine is a private tool used by one person (its owner) to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It signs in only with the owner's own Google account.

It uses the owner's YouTube, Google Sheets, Google Drive and Gmail data only to upload the owner's videos, record the channel's own statistics in the owner's private Google Sheet, and send the owner status emails. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.
