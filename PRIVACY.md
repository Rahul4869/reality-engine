# Privacy Policy – Reality Engine

Reality Engine is a private tool used by one person (its owner) to manage the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram professional account. It signs in only with the owner's own accounts.

It uses the owner's YouTube, Google Sheets, Google Drive and Gmail data, and the owner's Facebook Page and Instagram data, only to publish the owner's own videos and posts, to record the channels' own statistics in the owner's private Google Sheet, and to send the owner status emails. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No data about other people is stored.

## Data deletion

The tool stores nothing about anyone except its owner, so there is no other person's data to delete. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in Facebook Settings, Business Integrations, and in Google Account, Security, Third-party access. For any question about data, message the Reality Engine Page on Facebook.
